- Say that your project is **based on**, **forked from**, **compatible with** or **made for** Luminoctis.

- Publish a modified version, fork or derived project **named** Luminoctis, or with a name easily confused with it (for example “Luminoctis Pro”, “Luminoctis+”, “Lumi Noctis”).

If you fork the code, pick your own name and logo, and keep the MPL notices as the license requires.
